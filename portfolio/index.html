Featured Independent Software Project

Sound Control Lite

Native macOS Application · Swift · SwiftUI · CoreAudio

A packaged per-app audio mixer for macOS

I independently designed and built Sound Control Lite to solve a missing macOS control: changing one app's volume without altering the system volume. The menu-bar app provides per-app sliders, mute and reset controls, real-time audio activity detection, and a focused view of apps currently producing sound.

I developed the native SwiftUI interface and CoreAudio integration, packaged the app as a downloadable DMG, and iterated through public beta releases, including compatibility work for Zoom Share Sound routing.

Current Undergraduate Research

Single-cell aging and senescence

Develops computational workflows for single-cell RNA sequencing data—measurements of gene activity in individual cells—to characterize and predict aging- and senescence-related cellular states. The work builds structured, interpretable representations of biological variation and evaluates predictive models against simpler baselines, synthetic benchmarks, confounding checks, and independent datasets to distinguish transferable biological signal from dataset-specific or technical effects.

Responsibilities include biological data preparation, model evaluation, and running computational experiments on high-performance computing infrastructure. The project integrates ECE perspectives on measurement, noise, and systems with machine learning, computational biology, and single-cell biology. An AI-assisted, human-validated research workflow supports technical exploration, experiment planning, implementation, debugging, and documentation, while benchmarks, failure criteria, reproducible organization, and independent checks remain under human control.
