Why it exists

A lightweight mixer for the menu bar

macOS does not provide a simple built-in per-app volume mixer. Sound Control Lite brings focused audio controls to the menu bar without turning the app into a full audio suite.

Install

Install & Permissions

  1. 1
    Download the DMG

    Use the download button above to get SoundControlLite-v1.3.dmg.

  2. 2
    Open the DMG

    Double-click the downloaded DMG file.

  3. 3
    Drag to Applications

    Move SoundControlLite into your Applications folder.

  4. 4
    First launch

    If macOS blocks the app, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, then confirm Open.

  5. 5
    Grant Accessibility

    Go to Privacy & Security > Accessibility and turn on SoundControlLite.

  6. 6
    Grant System Audio Recording

    Go to Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording. Under System Audio Recording Only, turn on SoundControlLite.

  7. 7
    Restart SoundControlLite

    Quit and reopen the app after changing permissions.

SoundControlLite normally requires Accessibility and System Audio Recording Only. Full screen recording is not required unless macOS explicitly asks for it. Microphone permission is not normally required for basic per-app volume control.

Why Accessibility?

Allows SoundControlLite to interact with macOS app-level controls and provide reliable menu bar behavior.

Why System Audio Recording Only?

Allows SoundControlLite to detect system audio activity and control app audio safely.

FAQ

Short answers

Why does macOS warn that the developer cannot be verified?

Early beta builds may be unsigned or not notarized, so macOS may show a security warning.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

Accessibility can be required for protected app-level controls in the current implementation.

Why Screen Recording or Microphone?

macOS may require them for app/window association or audio activity detection.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit the app, remove it from Applications, and remove related privacy permissions if desired.
